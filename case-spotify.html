Case 04

Spotify: Composing the Future of Streaming

A scenario-planning study on how AI-generated music and shifting artist-monetization models could reshape Spotify's competitive position over the next five years.

Strategy / Scenario Planning Team Project STEEP Analysis GTM 3-Phase Strategy
Exhibit S — The Uncertainty

Two forces will decide the outcome

A STEEP scan of the music-streaming landscape surfaced two uncertainties that matter more than any other variable in play — how listeners feel about AI-made music, and whether artists get paid fairly for it.

SOCIAL — AI Acceptance

Will audiences embrace AI-generated music as a legitimate new form of creativity, or push back in favor of human-made content?

ECONOMIC — Artist Monetization

Will Spotify find a sustainable way to improve artist compensation, or will dissatisfaction push artists toward blockchain-based or direct-to-fan platforms?

Exhibit S2 — Competitive Landscape

Four futures, mapped against incumbents, insurgents, and adjacents

Exhibit C — Strategic Response

Key recommendations

0–1.5 YR

Hybrid AI + human content, with ethical guardrails

Launch Spotify Originals — exclusive human-made content enhanced by AI — alongside artist-facing AI tools and a new AI Ethics Board to get ahead of copyright and legal risk.

1.5–3 YR

Empower the creator economy

Direct-to-fan subscription tiers, NFT-backed music ownership, and AI-powered production and predictive-analytics tools for independent artists.

1.5–3 YR

AI-driven live and virtual experiences

AI-curated virtual concerts, interactive setlists, and Metaverse integrations on platforms like Meta and Roblox.

1.5–3 YR

Evolve the subscription model

Spotify Premium+: fan memberships, exclusive content drops, and hybrid real-world/digital artist interactions.

3–5 YR

Become the "Music Industry OS"

One platform for creation, monetization, and engagement — real-time AI remixing, AI-assisted collaboration, and a Spotify AI Studio that preserves artist rights.

Exhibit G — Go-to-Market Strategy

A three-phase GTM strategy to market leadership

Phase 1 · 0–1.5 yrs

Foundations & risk mitigation

  • Launch Spotify Originals — human-made, AI-enhanced curation
  • Roll out AI-augmented artist co-creation tools
  • Stand up the Spotify AI Ethics Board
  • Strengthen copyright-detection algorithms
Phase 2 · 1.5–3 yrs

Expansion & positioning

  • Direct-to-fan subscription tiers + NFT-backed ownership
  • AI-curated virtual concerts; Spotify × Metaverse (Meta, Roblox)
  • Artist services: mastering, remixing, Spotify Pro Insights
  • Multi-tier Spotify Premium+ with fan memberships
Phase 3 · 3–5 yrs

Market leadership

  • Fully integrated creation + monetization + engagement ecosystem
  • Creator-driven revenue models for licensing AI-enhanced music
  • Real-time AI remixing for dynamic listener interaction
  • Launch Spotify AI Studio with protected artist licensing
