Case 16 · Client & Advisory Work

BondCliq: AI Product Concepts for Corporate Bond Trading

AI product consulting for BondCliq, a fintech platform modernizing corporate bond trading through centralized pricing and market data.

AI Product Strategy Client Project Fintech GTM 3-Phase Strategy
Exhibit E — Engagement Context

Where AI fits into a still-fragmented market

Equities modernized first — electronic, transparent, high-speed. Corporate bonds remain more "pre-modern": bilateral, relationship-driven, and fragmented, with inconsistent quote data across dealers. BondCliq centralizes pricing and market data for the industry, creating a natural entry point to explore where AI could help close that information gap.

Exhibit R — Scope of Work

Research, then product concepts

The engagement combined research into how AI is being adopted across fixed-income trading with a practitioner survey of portfolio and investment managers, to gauge how ready the market actually is for an AI-driven solution. Most respondents were still early in their AI journey — which shaped a set of product concepts built around augmenting trader judgment with better, more consistent data, rather than pursuing full automation.

Exhibit F — Areas of Focus

Three concepts for filling the missing data layer

BOND SUMMARIZATION

Auto-generated, trade-ready summaries and comparables from dense bond data, so traders spend less time parsing information manually.

PRICE DISCOVERY

A quote-consistency concept for flagging stale or outlier quotes, paired with dealer analytics that score dealers on pricing and speed.

EXECUTION WORKFLOWS

Concepts for reducing friction between deciding to trade and completing it, with an eye toward future execution-venue integrations.

Exhibit G — Go-to-Market Strategy

A three-phase GTM strategy to build trust before scaling

Phase 1 · Foundation

Prove the concept with a small pilot group

  • Ship a focused MVP — quote-consistency checks and auto-generated bond summaries
  • Pilot with a small group of power users across asset managers and broker-dealers
  • Build compliance review into the process from day one
Phase 2 · Scale Intelligence

Broaden rollout as trust builds

  • Extend access to the full base of active users
  • Expand dealer analytics and quote-consistency detection
  • Introduce tiered access as adoption grows
Phase 3 · Ecosystem & Personalization

Extend the platform outward

  • Explore personalized dealer recommendations based on trading patterns
  • Pilot expansion into additional international markets
  • Explore integrations with execution venues and order-management systems
Exhibit P — Path Forward

Where the work goes from here

Beyond the initial concepts, the engagement pointed to a few ongoing priorities: strengthening the underlying data and infrastructure, deepening analytics as adoption grows, and — longer term — commercialization and expansion of the AI layer across more of the platform.
