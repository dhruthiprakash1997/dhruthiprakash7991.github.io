Case 05

Blue Origin: Building a Road to Space for the Benefit of Earth

Can Blue Origin strengthen its core, expand its business model, and achieve long-term industry leadership — despite execution delays and heavy dependence on government contracts?

Strategy / Scenario Planning Team Project STEEP Analysis GTM 3-Phase Strategy
Exhibit S — The Uncertainty

Two forces will decide the outcome

Blue Origin trails SpaceX on launch frequency and cost efficiency, and remains heavily reliant on NASA and DoD revenue. Two uncertainties determine whether it closes that gap.

ECONOMIC — Profitability of Space Ventures

The space economy is projected to grow from $630B to $1.8T by 2035 — but it's unclear whether space ventures can turn that growth into sustainable, long-term profit.

POLITICAL — Government Space Priorities

Shifting NASA/DoD budgets could redirect contracts toward in-house programs or dominant players like SpaceX, reshaping opportunity for everyone else.

Exhibit C — Strategic Response

Key recommendations

CORE

Stabilize government contract execution

Deliver Blue Moon MK1 and New Glenn on schedule to rebuild the credibility needed for future NASA and DoD contract wins.

EFFICIENCY

Advance propulsion & manufacturing

Scale additive manufacturing and next-generation hydrolox propulsion to cut production costs and improve reliability.

GROWTH

Diversify into commercial B2B services

Orbital Reef leasing, AWS-backed satellite infrastructure, and bundled launch-and-cloud packages reduce reliance on public funding.

TALENT

Build a collaborative innovation ecosystem

Club for the Future and the Blue Innovators Program bring in university and startup talent, with a path to acquire promising technology.

Exhibit G — Go-to-Market Strategy

A three-phase GTM strategy to industry leadership

Phase 1 · Present–2028

Establishing a sustainable core

  • Deliver Blue Moon MK1 for NASA's Artemis program on schedule
  • Ramp up New Glenn launch cadence, including Kuiper deployments
  • Advance additive manufacturing for engine production
  • Launch the Blue Innovators startup partnership program
Phase 2 · 2028–2031

Expanding the frontier

  • Deploy Orbital Reef in phases as a commercial research platform
  • Bundle launch, AWS ground-station, and AI data packages for satellite clients
  • Build launchpad & research infrastructure in key locations
  • Launch a rocket component retrieval & refurbishment service
Phase 3 · 2031+

Dominance in the new space economy

  • Monetize Orbital Reef through long-term commercial and government leasing
  • Pioneer in-space 3D printing and autonomous manufacturing
  • Develop extraterrestrial construction using lunar and Martian materials
