ShopSight × Gap: Bridging the Gap for Gen Z

A product-marketing hackathon pitch positioning ShopSight — an AI outfit-customization tool — as a consulting partner to help Gap close its innovation and Gen Z relevance gap. Placed 3rd.

Product Marketing Hackathon · 3rd Place Consulting Pitch Retail + AI
Exhibit E — Engagement Context

A heritage brand losing its relevance faster than its revenue

Gap has built a 55-year identity on timeless, affordable essentials, inclusive sizing, and everyday comfort for the whole family — but that same steadiness has left it exposed. The brand has posted consistent revenue and margin declines (a 16% drop in 2020 alone), with visible knock-on effects in store closures and thinner product assortments. The pitch treated this less as a demand problem and more as a signal-and-speed problem: Gap wasn't hearing what its next customer wanted fast enough to act on it.

Exhibit F — Where the Gap Actually Is

Four gaps, one root cause: slow signal-to-shelf

INNOVATION

Limited innovation growth — new lines lag behind what's already trending elsewhere.

IDENTITY

Lack of a unique brand identity distinct from other basics-first competitors.

GEN Z

Struggling to capture the Gen Z audience that now sets fast-fashion demand signals.

COMPETITION

An intense competitive landscape with little room for a slow-to-react incumbent.

Exhibit S — The ShopSight Solution

Using AI-prompted outfit data to see the gap before you stock it

ShopSight lets shoppers generate and customize outfits from AI prompts, which — as a byproduct — generates a live map of exactly what customers are asking for that a retailer isn't offering. A McKinsey study cited in the pitch found that fashion brands using data to forecast trends see a 10–30% increase in innovation speed; the pitch translated that into two concrete, side-by-side product gaps ShopSight surfaced for Gap.

GAP 01

Cocktail wear

Gap's offering skews toward a simple, boxy shift dress. ShopSight's prompt data showed Gen Z gravitating toward a fitted, corseted mini silhouette — a specific construction detail Gap's current line doesn't cover.

GAP 02

Maternity wear

Gap's maternity dress is a classic long-sleeve knit. Gen Z moms' prompts pointed to an off-shoulder, ribbed two-piece set — same comfort proposition, different silhouette and styling language.

Exhibit R — Recommendations

What a ShopSight partnership would let Gap act on

  • Develop more focused, going-out and occasion wear for Gen Z rather than basics-only assortments
  • Enhance digital and omni-channel presence around the prompt-driven discovery ShopSight enables
  • Reduce dead stock by producing closer to validated silhouette demand instead of forecasted trend
  • Expand sustainable product lines like Gap-Green, an area ShopSight data showed had underserved demand
