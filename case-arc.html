Case 11 · Marketing Analysis

ARC: Pricing & Promotion for an Athletic Recovery Studio

Real client work for Bucked Up Recovery (ARC) — a pricing, positioning, and go-to-market plan covering STP, service pricing, promotional strategy, and break-even economics.

Marketing Analysis Client Project STP + 4Ps Break-Even Analysis
Exhibit STP — Two Segments, Two Value Props

Who ARC serves, and why they come back

CORE SERVICES — AGES 25–55, ALL GENDERS

Job: help relax the mind and body. Pain: stress, overwork, costly services. Gain: stress relief at a reasonable price.

HYPERBARIC — ATHLETES & SPORTSPEOPLE, ALL AGES

Job: muscle recovery. Pain: costly, time-consuming recovery options. Gain: convenient, quick, affordable cell rejuvenation.

Exhibit P — Pricing Recommendations

Three offers to lower the barrier to trial and repeat visits

BUNDLE

New 5-session core bundle — $100

Five sessions across each core service, plus one bonus cold-plunge and one bonus sauna session.

SEASONAL

Holiday hyperbaric bundle

10% off five hyperbaric sessions, shareable across up to 5 users — a seasonal acquisition offer built for gifting.

CROSS-SELL

1-for-1 hyperbaric-to-core offer

One free core session with every hyperbaric session booked — promotes both physical and mental well-being and the ARC community.

Exhibit G — Promotional Strategy

Building local, credible demand

REFERRAL

Local partnerships

Partner with Bull City Physical Therapy, fitness influencers, and chiropractors, PTs, and orthopedists for patient referrals.

AWARENESS

Localized reach

Targeted Spotify audio ads, local-keyword SEO, and free or low-cost recovery workshops at UNC and around Cary.

VISIBILITY

Event sponsorship

Sponsor local marathons, triathlons, and sports tournaments to reach the athlete segment directly.

RETAIL

Renegotiated Bucked Up partnership

Shift from a flat 40% sales cut to a shared-profit model, bundling ARC recovery services with Bucked Up's retail products.
