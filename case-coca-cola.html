Case 09 · Individual Project

Coca-Cola: Navigating the Pressure Points

Health, sustainability, and future resilience — how a 139-year-old legacy brand modernizes without losing the identity that built it.

Strategy / Scenario Planning Individual Project STEEP Analysis GTM 3-Phase Strategy
Exhibit S — The Uncertainty

Two forces will decide the outcome

Coca-Cola posted $47.1B in 2024 revenue and 3% growth — but sits at an inflection point. Two uncertainties determine what the next decade of the beverage industry looks like.

SOCIAL — Health-Conscious Consumer Behavior

Will mainstream demand stay loyal to sugary, familiar favorites, or pivot decisively toward clean-label, functional, low-calorie drinks?

POLITICAL — Regulatory Pressure on Packaging & Waste

Plastic, water, and carbon rules are tightening unevenly worldwide. Will enforcement stay soft and fragmented, or turn strict and coordinated?

Exhibit C — Strategic Response

Key recommendations

TRUST

Rebuild trust with an ESG reset

Publicly recommit to science-based targets for plastic reduction, water neutrality, and carbon transparency — signal change before mandates force it.

BRAND

Reignite brand love with AI, carefully

Revive campaigns like "Share a Coke" with AI-powered personalization, paired with transparent governance to avoid data-misuse backlash.

PORTFOLIO

Expand wellness SKUs, protect the core

Launch clean-label and functional lines under sub-brands while leaving Classic Coke untouched — innovate at the edges, not the center.

SUPPLY CHAIN

Pilot regenerative packaging early

Co-invest in refill and recycling infrastructure in high-regulation markets before compliance becomes mandatory and costly.

Exhibit G — Go-to-Market Strategy

A three-phase GTM strategy, from defending the core to leading the transformation

Phase 1 · 0–2 yrs

Defend the core

  • Launch an ESG Reset Campaign with science-based plastic, water & carbon targets
  • Pilot refillable packaging infrastructure in EU and Canada
  • Revive "Share a Coke" with Gen AI–powered, regional personalization
  • Deploy AI-powered marketing labs for predictive consumer insight
Phase 2 · 3–5 yrs

Expand into growth platforms

  • Launch clean-label, prebiotic & functional SKUs alongside an untouched Classic Coke
  • Deploy AI vending machines personalized by weather, events & foot traffic
  • Pilot regenerative packaging — algae-based, compostable — in high-regulation markets
  • Co-locate bottling and recycling plants to close material loops
Phase 3 · 5+ yrs

Lead the transformation

  • Launch a modular "Coca-Cola Wellness Cloud" — on-demand, AI-personalized beverages
  • Build biometric data partnerships (Apple Health, WHOOP, Amazon Wellness)
  • Achieve net-zero and water-positive status; scale refillable stations in 30+ markets
  • Position Coca-Cola as a "Good Growth" brand — health equity and environmental restoration
