Case 07

Illumina: The Sequence of the Future

Navigating AI-powered genomics — how technological disruption and shifting public perception could drive Illumina's transformation into the central platform for precision medicine.

Strategy / Scenario Planning Team Project STEEP Analysis Platform Ecosystem
Exhibit S — The Uncertainty

Two forces will decide the outcome

AI is already reshaping genomics. Two uncertainties determine whether that reshaping benefits patients — or just a handful of platforms.

TECHNOLOGICAL — Pace of AI Advancement

Open-source models like DeepSeek-R1 and NVIDIA's GPU advances are accelerating real-time sequencing — but how fast legacy players adapt remains unclear.

ECONOMIC — Cost vs. Demand

Healthcare and research costs rose 8% YoY in 2025. Whether AI lowers costs enough to reach mass-market demand — or keeps genomics a premium service — is still open.

Exhibit C — Strategic Response

Key recommendations

PIVOT

Invest in AI-powered preventative healthcare

Shift the value proposition from diagnostics to prediction and prevention, reducing long-term system costs.

PARTNER

Expand genomics + AI partnerships

Deepen alliances with cloud and compute providers — NVIDIA, AWS, Oracle — plus pharma, to co-develop AI-driven solutions.

POLICY

Help shape AI regulatory frameworks

Proactively engage regulators to balance innovation with data privacy and compliance, rather than reacting to rules after the fact.

SCALE

Optimize AI-driven cost efficiency

Use AI to shorten hospital stays and streamline R&D so genomic solutions stay financially viable for mass adoption.

Exhibit P — Platform Ecosystem

Becoming the orchestrator, not just a supplier

Illumina's strongest path is to sit at the center of a genomics ecosystem — owning the platform that connects the people who produce data, provide infrastructure, and consume the results.

PRODUCERS

Pharma & biotech firms manufacturing precision therapies; AI developers (OpenAI, DeepMind) supplying models; genome labs & universities deepening sequencing research.

PLATFORM — OWNER: ILLUMINA

The centralized ecosystem combining AI, cloud computing, and genomic sequencing — orchestrating predictive, preventative, and precision medicine.

CONSUMERS

Patients and genomics startups (23andMe); hospitals & clinics integrating diagnostics; regulators like the FDA overseeing approvals and compliance.
