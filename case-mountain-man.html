Case 13 · Marketing Analysis

Mountain Man Brewing: Should They Launch a Light Beer?

A breakeven-driven recommendation on whether a loyal, regional lager brand should risk diluting itself by entering the crowded light beer market.

Marketing Analysis Team Project Breakeven Analysis Go/No-Go Recommendation
Exhibit S — The Situation

A loyal brand facing its first real shift in 50 years

BRAND CONTEXT

Consumer preferences shifted toward younger buyers for the first time in 50 years. Mountain Man's bitter, distinctive taste built fierce loyalty through off-premise retail and liquor stores — a channel that doesn't reach the bars and restaurants where younger drinkers actually are.

PRODUCT RISK

A light beer means fighting entrenched national brands with far bigger budgets, running two products at once, and risking the loyalty of the existing customer base in the process.

Exhibit C — Recommendation

Don't launch — reinvest in what already works

DECISION

Do not introduce Mountain Man Light

The breakeven volume exceeds any realistic market share, and development costs outweigh the return of simply investing in the existing brand.

REINVEST

Market MM Lager as the authentic local beer

Redirect the light-beer budget into positioning MM Lager as a "local" brand — younger drinkers actually favor smaller regional labels.

DISTRIBUTION

Expand into on-premise locations

Grow into the restaurants and bars where younger drinkers actually are, paired with TV ads and local festival sponsorships.

FOOTPRINT

Stay regional

Focus on the East Central Region rather than national expansion, protecting manufacturing capacity and brand allure.
