Case 06

Shaping the Future of Robotics: Boston Dynamics' New Chapter

A technical pioneer with a commercialization problem — can Boston Dynamics scale revenue while Tesla and Chinese manufacturers undercut it on price?

Strategy / Scenario Planning Team Project SCQ + STEEP GTM 3-Phase Strategy
Exhibit S — The Uncertainty

Two forces will decide the outcome

Boston Dynamics has technical leadership but a hard time monetizing it. Two uncertainties will decide whether that changes.

ECONOMIC — Affordability & Purchasing Power

Competitors like Tesla and Agility Robotics are chasing sub-$20K price points. Will middle-class and business budgets stretch to justify robotics ROI?

POLITICAL — Government Regulation

Safety, liability, and AI data-privacy rules are still being written. Stricter regulation could delay certification and consumer-facing launches.

Exhibit C — Strategic Response

Key recommendations

FOCUS

Prioritize B2B & defense customers

Target enterprise and defense buyers who are less price-sensitive, using Spot, Stretch, and Atlas to build near-term revenue and credibility.

MODEL

Launch Robotics-as-a-Service

Flexible leasing and subscription contracts lower the upfront cost barrier and unlock SME adoption without a full hardware purchase.

POLICY

Engage regulators early

Help shape emerging safety and data-privacy standards so products stay compliant and market-ready as rules solidify.

COST

Push toward a sub-$20K price point

Invest in R&D and component partnerships — NVIDIA, Hyundai, Amazon — to close the cost gap with Tesla Optimus and Chinese manufacturers.

Exhibit G — Go-to-Market Strategy

A three-phase GTM strategy to ecosystem leadership

Phase 1 · 0–2 yrs

Short-term survival & consolidation

  • Drive revenue from proven products — Spot, Stretch, Atlas
  • Prioritize B2B & defense over the price-sensitive consumer market
  • Introduce Robotics-as-a-Service leasing
  • Begin regulatory engagement on safety & data privacy
Phase 2 · 2–5 yrs

Market expansion & differentiation

  • Scale production toward a sub-$20K price point on select lines
  • Diversify into modular products for industrial & early consumer use
  • Integrate generative AI and fleet management (Orbit)
  • Expand into markets with more flexible regulation
Phase 3 · 5+ yrs

Ecosystem leadership

  • Shift from hardware maker to open robotics platform
  • Pursue mass-market B2B and B2C penetration
  • Launch platform subscriptions and data-driven business models
  • Invest in sustainable production & end-of-life recycling
