Taskmates: Teammates Who Actually Do the Tasks

An AI-powered group-project coordinator that splits tasks, balances workload, and gives professors evidence-based contribution data — built to kill the free-rider problem in student group work.

AI Product Design 1st Place · Duke Prelims Women in Product Hackathon EdTech

Exhibit E — Engagement Context

Won at Duke, then took Duke to the MEMPC finals

Taskmates was built for the Women in Product · Duke University Hackathon (2025), where it placed 1st in the prelims against other Duke teams. That result earned the team a spot representing Duke at the MEMPC intercollegiate finals, where a new prototype — Nest — was built and taken to 2nd runner-up (see Case 17).

Exhibit R — Scope of Work

Depth over breadth for a 48-hour build

The scope stayed intentionally tight for the prelim window: one core loop (AI task splitting → workload balancing → nudges → FairGrade export) built and demoed end-to-end, rather than a wider feature set left half-working. That focus is what the persona work and go-to-market plan below are built around.

Exhibit F — Product

Six features, one accountability layer

AI TASK SPLITTER

Paste the project brief; AI generates a balanced task breakdown in seconds — no more arguing about who does what.

WORKLOAD BALANCER

Estimates hours per task and distributes them evenly so no single teammate carries a disproportionate load.

SMART NUDGE SYSTEM

Automated reminders come from the app, not a teammate, when tasks are overdue — accountability without the awkward text.

MEETING NOTES PARSER

Paste Zoom notes and AI extracts action items, assigns owners, and adds them straight to the task board.

CONTRIBUTION TRACKER

A real-time, timestamped log of who did what — visible to the team and exportable to the professor.

FAIRGRADE REPORT

A submission-time PDF of task completion, on-time rates, and per-person contribution — evidence-based grading.

Exhibit S — Go-to-Market

An institutional wedge, not a cold B2C launch

ACQUIRE

Professor-led entry, not student-by-student

One professor recommending Taskmates brings ~30 students per class — an institutional entry point that scales faster than pure B2C, paired with a campus ambassador program and a viral FairGrade share loop on LinkedIn and Instagram.

RETAIN

Built around the academic calendar

Semester-arc design, contribution streaks, and weekly recap emails keep engagement tied to syllabus week, midterms, and finals — with the professor's use of FairGrade reports as the strongest retention driver, since students know accountability is real.

MONETIZE

Freemium → Premium → Institutional

Free for any .edu login, $4.99/mo or $29/semester for AI parsing and FairGrade export, and custom department-wide licensing with an admin analytics portal and Canvas LMS integration for institutions.
